## One evidence system, three presentation routes

- Research Lab is the core product: supported-area selection, automatic calendar,
  qualified historical finding, optional evidence inspection and export.
- The short P0 demo presents one frozen, independently replayed case from that same
  system. Sixty to ninety seconds is a design target, not a measured guarantee.
- Story Mode is optional: Parallax -> satellite perspectives -> Third Eye review.
  Its completion is never required to use the Lab or inspect a result.

The landing view provides immediate Lab access and clear mode choices without a
forced introduction, animation, sound or scientific parameter selection. Do not
advertise unfinished modes as working. Direct Lab and evidence links bypass the
lobby. Optional art failure must not block the scientific interface.

## Meaning of the visual concept

Parallax represents different observation perspectives. Explicit MODIS, VIIRS
and THE THIRD EYE controls work with keyboard, touch and mouse. Decorative motion
does not move real geographic observations or imply a physical sensor model.
No eye tracking, camera access or promise of eye-closure detection is required.

Third Eye means reviewing the comparison, not observing hidden truth. Its reveal
opens the qualified result, applicability checks and remaining uncertainty.
It cannot imply recovered missed fires, perfect harmonization or a third sensor.
Use comparable overlap windows for a direct sensor illustration; keep these
distinct from the historical periods in a cross-era question.

## Shared state and scientific meaning

All modes use the same immutable case/artifact version, geometry ID, periods,
measurement definition, support policy and calibration/evaluation identifiers.
Mode switches preserve this state. Missing or stale artifacts are explicit;
do not silently substitute a different flagship or another area's calibration.

Raw availability and evaluated comparison availability are different geographic
layers. Map clipping changes presentation only. Subsets/unions must satisfy the
current plan's geometry and applicability rules. Do not blend raw platform series
into a harmonized trend or average regional rates.

Keep these dimensions visible and separately represented:

- Calibration sensitivity across registered variants.
- Applicability, including actual failed checks versus absent/out-of-domain checks.
- Inference status, normally `not_established`.
- Practical-change category when a justified margin exists, alongside magnitude.

Do not inherit the archived frontend's combined UNRESOLVED enum for both missing
evidence and observed disagreement. Use the current scientific schema. A stable
variant set can fail applicability; within a practical margin is not proof of
equivalence. Plain-language templates must preserve these distinctions.

## Narrative and evidence rules

Story, Quick Mission, Lab and exports show identical quantities and limitations.
Any game-like decisions change presentation only, never scientific results.
Confirmation, qualification and unresolved evidence are all legitimate endings.
Do not force a reversal or a successful Evidence Repair scene. If optional repair
is shown, retain its eligibility, experimental status and model-inadequacy outcome.

Automatic findings use the registered comparison-selection rules. Every statement
links to its evidence artifact; all eligible outcomes remain accessible. Separate
fictional framing from NASA observations and clearly mark synthetic examples.

## Frontend acceptance

- First screen has area, calendar, one finding, one limit and a plain status chip;
  the landing page shows the frozen headline metric and denominator, or its
  unavailable status. No internal schema terms on the first screen or video.
- The P0 FIRMS link reflects the selected area/date where supported and explains
  any manual selection; the two-sentence grid-versus-points explainer is visible.
- P3/P5 produce the one-page results, demo script and raw screen capture by Sunday
  20:00, using the same actual results and limitations as the site.

- Lab and export remain usable without Story, audio, WebGL or a runtime model.
- Keyboard/reduced-motion paths expose the same scientific information.
- Region changes cannot reuse another area's factor, cache or baseline.
- One case has identical values and statuses in every implemented mode/export.
- Stable-but-failed, missing-required-evidence, raw-only, unavailable and
  within-margin opposite-sign fixtures receive accurate wording.
- The demo does not imply a live analysis when serving a frozen artifact.
- Every narrative evidence panel links directly to its Lab equivalent.

This bridge adds no scientific subsystem or requirement to complete elaborate
Story Mode. P3 owns presentation; P1/P2 review scientific wording, P4 owns shared
contracts and P5 verifies parity and accessibility.
