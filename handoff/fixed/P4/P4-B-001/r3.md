## 1. Scientific Rules & Schema Reconciliation

### A. Adverse Check Preservation Under Incomplete Evidence (P1/P2 Approval Requested)
- **Problem Fixed**: When an applicability check failed and a required variant or evidence support was missing, `expected_output_category` previously returned `"not_enough_usable_evidence"`, hiding the failed check.
- **Rule Enforced**:
  - Adverse applicability check failures (`failed_applicability`) must never be obscured under a generic missing evidence label.
  - When both conditions occur simultaneously (`applicability == "failed_applicability"` and `not complete`):
    - Output category is deterministically: `"calibration_check_failed_and_missing_evidence"`.
    - Plain-language status label is: `"Calibration check failed and required evidence missing"`.
    - Both facts are explicitly preserved in the summary and claim audit record.
    - Hiding the failed check under `"not_enough_usable_evidence"` or hiding the missing evidence under `"calibration_check_failed"` is rejected by contract validation.
    - `conclusion_eligible` remains strictly `False`.

### B. Observed Sensitivity Preservation & Practical Margin (Contract 1.1.0)
- When required variants are missing (`complete=False`), observed sensitivity across available variants is preserved as `sensitivity_status = "sensitive_to_assumptions"` rather than forced to `"unavailable"`. Stability (`"stable_across_displayed_settings"`) remains strictly forbidden when variants are missing.
- When effects fall within the registered practical margin $\tau$, `headline_direction` is set to `"within_practical_margin"` instead of `"no_change"`, preventing false implication of zero effect. Exact directional signs of all variants are preserved separately in `practical_change.observed_signs`. `headline_direction = "no_change"` is forbidden when non-zero deltas exist.

### C. Five-Field Cache-Key Requirement for Consumers (P1, P2, P3, P5 Coordination)
- Cache key definition: `(artifact_version, geometry_id, period_spec, method_id, policy_id)`.
- Consumers (frontend cache, evaluation harness, manifest validators) must key all artifact storage and lookup operations with all five fields.
- Sub-areas cannot inherit or borrow parent geometry cache entries.
- Validated via `assert_cache_key_match` in `contracts/schemas/common.py` and `cache_key_sha256` in manifests.
