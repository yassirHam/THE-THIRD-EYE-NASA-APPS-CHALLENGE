  - `partial_period_reason = "snpp_record_ended_2026_11_02"`
- Enforced declared `week_definition` in `WeeklyCalendarSeries`:
  - When `week_definition == "utc_iso_week"`, all records must strictly begin on Monday (`weekday == 0`), end on Sunday (`weekday == 6`, exactly 6 days later), and have `(year, week_number)` matching `start_date.isocalendar()`. Arbitrary bins (e.g. Tuesday-to-Monday) are strictly rejected.
  - When `week_definition == "fixed_7_day_bins"`, records must strictly align with Jan 1 + (k-1)*7 days, cannot exceed week 52, and cannot cross year boundaries.
  - All records in a weekly series are strictly validated to prevent overlapping date ranges.
- S-NPP weeks starting after cessation (`2026-11-09` onwards) are strictly rejected as `after_record`.

### B. F-02 Resolution: Null Label Representation for Partial Periods & Decision Guards
- `MonthRecord.unusual_status` and `WeekRecord.critical_status`/`unusual_status` now permit `None` (`null` in JSON serialization).
- For partial periods:
  - Decisions are suppressed: labels must be `null` (`None`) or `"not_applicable"` sentinel. Normal decisions (`"unusual"`, `"not_unusual"`, `"critical"`, `"not_critical"`, `"insufficient_baseline"`) are strictly rejected.
  - The canonical fixture `contracts/fixtures/sample_calendar.json` now explicitly serializes `null` for partial month `unusual_status` and partial week `critical_status`/`unusual_status`.
- For complete periods:
  - Available complete periods strictly require concrete decisions and reject `null` and `"not_applicable"`.
  - Unavailable complete periods (`availability == "unavailable"`) cannot claim `"unusual"`, `"not_unusual"`, `"critical"`, or `"not_critical"`; decisions require available observations. Non-decisions (`"insufficient_baseline"` or `null`) are required.
- Clarified contract and acceptance representation:
  - Monthly records carry `baseline_median` and `baseline_p90` which are strictly `null` for partial months.
  - Weekly records carry `critical_status` and `unusual_status` which are strictly `null` (or `"not_applicable"`) for partial weeks; weekly records do not carry monthly baseline thresholds.
