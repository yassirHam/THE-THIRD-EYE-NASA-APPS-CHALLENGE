Enhancements applied to the authoritative geographic-access plan:

1. Exact geographic identity: no silent polygon-to-parent substitution, inherited
   subset/union calibration, average-of-rates aggregation or partial-footprint
   conclusions. Register boundary rules, deduplicate cells and show actual scope.
2. Automatic-summary selection: frozen ordering/eligibility and access to all
   comparisons. A selected extreme is not an inferential discovery. No invented
   queued jobs or dramatic fallback findings.
3. Failed applicability is distinct from missing evidence and out-of-domain
   use. Templates preserve this even when calibration variants agree.
4. Restore an outcome-neutral flagship and Evidence Repair's ability to challenge
   all scenarios, rather than making either feature depend on a dramatic reversal.
5. Clarify user-study blinding/order effects and remove the dangling reference to
   collection identifiers in an old execution specification.

Remaining scientific limits are substantive: wider raw coverage does not validate
more regions; a recent raw month does not establish transfer; a stable scalar
audit can share systematic bias; automatic text does not create statistical
support. The plan must disclose these even with perfect implementation.

The current operational documents now point to one plan. The older research plan,
award/readiness documents, PDFs and visual proposals are archived, not deleted.
InstaBox is documented as an optional bounded Linux workspace; cloud availability
does not strengthen scientific inference or guarantee delivery.
