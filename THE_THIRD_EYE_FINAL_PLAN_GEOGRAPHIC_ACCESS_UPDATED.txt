1. DECISION AND PURPOSE

Build a usable historical calendar during the event using verified NASA data sources.
The comparison tool is its distinctive analytical capability, not a separate
research interface that users must understand before exploring the data. Begin
with registered-factor sensitivity checks. A richer joint-constraint engine is
conditional on justified assumptions and demonstrated additional value.
Do not promise recovery of actual missed fires or a new physical detection theory.

Main workflow (default is automatic, not a do-it-yourself research console):
Choose an eligible area -> THE THIRD EYE loads a validated/precomputed calendar ->
automatically identifies registered seasonal patterns and eligible comparisons ->
checks calibration stability, support and applicability -> displays a plain-English
finding with its limits -> user may open evidence and export. Optional manual
comparisons use the same frozen scientific rules; they do not bypass validity
gates. No user has to choose a correction factor or search archives by hand.

Primary question:
Which historical seasonal comparisons survive the registered sensor-transfer
and observation-support assumptions, and which admit conflicting answers?

Potential users and decisions (illustrative, not recruited partners):
- A forest or fire department planner reviews past seasonal timing to prepare a
  next-season staffing calendar; a false sensor-change trend could misdirect staffing.
- An air-quality agency analyst reviews burning seasons to prioritize retrospective
  air-quality investigation; a false trend could send analysis toward the wrong years.
- A journalist or NGO checks a public claim that fires are increasing; confusing a
  sensor seam for a trend could lead to a misleading account. None is a forecast.
P5 finds and cites one real published regional claim or report during the event;
verify its measurement and relevance. Do not imply participation or adoption.

One-line pitch (repeat across submission materials):
"The fire calendar that tells you when a satellite change, not the fire, made
the difference - and when it can't tell." This is the intended question, not a
claim of causal identification; results describe reference-relative comparisons.

Capability comparison with a typical harmonized calendar (verify any external
comparison; these are intended capabilities, not claims about named teams):
- Shows raw and corrected comparisons with a held-out sensor-switch benchmark.
- Separates calibration sensitivity, applicability failure and missing evidence.
- Links each qualified finding to versioned inputs and independent replay.

User-first scientific promise:
- The user chooses an available geography, not a calibration method.
- The app automatically presents an honest historical finding and an evidence
  status; a chart never silently substitutes for an evaluated conclusion.
- The user can inspect NASA source records, sensitivity and reproduction steps
  as optional drill-down. Experts retain control over interpretation.
- A simple default is the entry point; technical complexity stays accessible,
  but secondary. Unsupported regions/periods display reasons, not invented data.

Research hypothesis:
Mandatory Stage A hypothesis: under a preregistered artificial sensor handover
on untouched overlap observations, calibration and registered sensitivity checks
reduce reference-relative mistaken directional conclusions at useful decision
coverage compared with raw comparisons and strong uncertainty-aware baselines.
Report a null or negative result if that is what occurs; this is a required
experiment, not a guaranteed beneficial finding.

Conditional extension hypothesis: a joint sensor-reference analysis can reduce incorrect
decisive conclusions relative to a held-out same-sensor reference while retaining
useful decision coverage, compared with raw charts and strong uncertainty-aware
harmonization baselines.

This is a hypothesis, not a performance claim. Best Use of Data is the target,
not a forecast. Partial identification, sensitivity analysis, robust optimization
and breakdown frontiers are established methods. Potential novelty is in the
validated application and useful evidence product, not those mathematical ideas.
Best Use of Science is a secondary fit based on demonstrated scientific method,
not a requirement to deliver Stage B or invent a new mathematical theory.
Keeping an unjustified method optional is a safeguard, not an award defect.

2026 NASA Space Apps judging alignment (evidence, not score promises):
- Impact: demonstrate one real retrospective analyst decision the calendar helps
  qualify, without claiming emergency forecasts or policy consequences.
- Creativity: document concrete differences from ordinary raw and calibrated
  calendars and show why they matter, not invented algorithmic originality.
- Validity: independently replay NASA-derived results and predeclared held-out
  benchmark errors, exclusions, and answer coverage.
- Relevance: deliver the requested MODIS/VIIRS hotspot-based, historical,
  selected-area calendar and explicitly map any unmet requirements.
- Presentation: users and judges can grasp the result within one interaction;
  linked evidence supports each visual or spoken claim.
These five criteria are reported by 2026 Space Apps local-event guidance; verify
current official judging/submission rules before finalizing the submission.

Seasonal baseline (P0 monthly; freeze parameters at kickoff before outcomes):
For each calendar month and sensor/policy, use eligible prior years in that same
sensor era, excluding the target year and all later years. Register the first and
last baseline years and a minimum count at kickoff from available source coverage;

rank ceil(0.90*n)). Values strictly above the percentile are unusual; ties at the
threshold are not. If the median is zero, report the absolute difference only;
percentage change is undefined. Do not treat missing or O=0 as zero. No baseline
crosses sensors without a separately evaluated mapping at that resolution.

  show insufficient baseline if requirements fail, not a fabricated season.
- "Critical weeks": the registered busiest weeks of the typical year, computed

4A. OBSERVATION-OPPORTUNITY AND MASK AUDIT [P0 core; P1 expanded]

P2/P1 document what the selected daily composites retain and discard. A usable
cell-day is not an overpass count: different numbers or times of acquisitions
can change the chance of a retained detection while O counts the day once.
R is normalized by usable cell-days, not fully standardized observation effort.
Even identical jointly usable cell-days need not represent matched acquisitions.
Do not interpret calibration as removing these differences automatically.

Record per product/version: compositing selection rules, relevant QA semantics,
missingness, qualifying date sets and spatial footprints, and whether acquisition
number/timing is observable or unknown. Audit available day/night categories
only within their documented composite semantics; they do not recover all passes.
The VNP14A1 sample band is a swath sample index, not an observation counter.
Nominal orbit schedules do not prove usable acquisitions. Report unknowns in the
calendar methodology and evidence package; inability to count opportunities from
daily grids is a limitation, not a reason to invent a denominator or block an
otherwise valid descriptive calendar.

Explicit product-specific mask fixtures are required before calibration: verify
fire-confidence classes, usable non-fire land, cloud, water, unclassified/unknown,
not-processed, fill/masked pixels and VIIRS bowtie deletion against the exact
collection documentation. Never share a decoder merely because class numbers
look similar. Combine the frozen land mask and QA rules consistently; excluded
low-confidence detections are not no-fire. Hand-check D/O accounting on small
examples plus real pixel extracts. Capture unknown/reserved classes explicitly.

4B. GEOGRAPHIC COMPOSITION - ACCOUNTING AND FIXTURE [P1; mandatory if used]

P2 preserves D_b,t and O_b,t by fixed, nonoverlapping geographic block b, sensor,
period and policy, alongside boundary/grid IDs, exclusions and eligibility.
Blocks partition the study region under registered inclusion rules; block counts
must sum exactly to published regional counts. Use suitable documented spatial units rather than introduce another global pipeline. Choose units and candidate
fixed weights from geography/development feasibility before evaluation outcomes.

Let r_b,t=1000*D_b,t/O_b,t and w_b,t=O_b,t/sum_b(O_b,t). Then, for observed
eligible support, R_t=sum_b(w_b,t*r_b,t). Thus the regional index changes when
the geographical mixture of usable observations changes, even if block rates
are unchanged. Normalizing by total usable cell-days does not remove that effect.
This is composition/confounding in aggregation, related to Simpson's paradox;
it is not necessarily a strict direction-reversal example of that paradox.

Mandatory hand-computed fixture: block rates 10 and 1, usable-observation shares
0.8/0.2 versus 0.2/0.8, produce 8.2 versus 2.8 (65.85% decrease), while fixed
0.5/0.5 weights give 5.5 in both periods. Assert the weighted-sum identity and
count reconciliation. Also test a missing required block and zero usable support:
never substitute zero or renormalize remaining fixed weights silently.

Conditional standardization uses R_std,t=sum_b(q_b*r_b,t), where q is fixed,
nonnegative and sums to one over a registered target geography. Equal weights
are illustrative, not a universal default. Eligible land/grid-cell shares are
a possible choice when justified by the target; record why the weights fit the
question. Omit the result if a required block lacks prespecified support, or use
a separately registered, identically restricted target in both periods with its
own label. Do not select the restricted geography to obtain a preferred outcome.

R_std is a different estimand: fixed-geography aggregation of observed block
rates. It does not recover missing fire activity or solve within-block/cloud/time
selection. Keep original and standardized series visible with distinct IDs.
Any mapped standardized series needs its own development-only calibration,
baseline and evaluation, or a separately justified transfer rule. Never silently
apply the original own-support factor or splice common-support estimates into
this new historical target. A contextual standardized disagreement cannot
automatically overturn the primary own-support verdict; report it as composition
sensitivity of the broader interpretation, with measurement differences explicit.

6. STAGED SCIENTIFIC IMPLEMENTATION

Stage A - minimum comparison method, no solver required:
For each registered policy, fit the full-training factor defined below and repeat
after leaving out each training year. Keep the own-support historical factor
separate from the common-support diagnostic factor. Recompute each comparison
under each applicable factor, with one shared factor for same-policy VIIRS
values on both sides. Preserve the policy-specific baseline and sample rules.

Factor definition [P0]: for the same registered training years and matched
calendar strata, calculate each sensor's pooled rate as 1000*sum(D)/sum(O),
with sums across eligible training observations before division. The own-support
factor a_H is pooled Aqua own-support rate divided by pooled VIIRS own-support
rate; the diagnostic a_C uses each sensor's pooled rate on the identical paired
common-support cell-days. Each leave-one-year-out factor repeats the complete
calculation after excluding that training year. Apply the factor to VIIRS rates;
never average regional rates or ratios. Zero O, zero VIIRS rate, inadequate
registered support, or a nonpositive/undefined factor means unavailable, not a
made-up factor. Freeze strata, masks and minimum support at kickoff; record them.
Common support is diagnostic and does not replace the historical own-support target.

Label agreement 'Stable across the registered calibration variants'. These
variants test training-year composition, not all plausible observation biases,
timing shifts or historical transfer failures. A required undefined factor
remains unavailable and follows unresolved precedence; never drop it to get a
stable badge. Show the number and identity of evaluated/required variants.

The min-max factor envelope is not a confidence interval or a physical bound.
Interpolation between fitted factors is an additional sensitivity assumption;
it does not make every intermediate value an empirically established estimate.
Do not label Stage A 'robust to all plausible sensor corrections'.

Stage B - conditional joint-constraint extension:
Begin only after Stage A and the calendar work, and development evidence
justifies studying additional structure. Freeze the proposed model and success
criteria before held-out comparison. Retain it in the delivered scientific
claim only if evaluation establishes added value over simpler alternatives.

Start with a sensor-reference sensitivity model, not a latent-fire simulator.
For a fixed policy and reference-domain stratum, a candidate formulation is:

  Z_m = a * V_m + e_m

V_m is the observed VIIRS indicator; Z_m is a candidate Aqua-reference-scale
interpretation; a is one shared positive mapping parameter; e_m represents a
registered transfer-discrepancy allowance. Aqua periods can be observed anchors
for this reference task, not error-free measurements of actual fire activity.

This is a candidate for the extension gate, not a preapproved physical
model. Start with a shared scalar and zero discrepancy as the simplest baseline.
Add discrepancy allowances only when development residuals justify studying
them, with their meaning and transfer assumptions stated explicitly.

For each policy, P1 registers:
- How the point factor and admissible factor range are constructed.
- How discrepancy allowances are derived from development residuals, with
  season/coverage conditioning only where adequate support exists.
- Which parameters are shared across months and comparisons.
- Any externally justified dependencies or joint constraints.
- Applicable periods, support minima, extrapolation limits and failure states.

An envelope of development residuals or leave-one-year-out factors is an
empirical sensitivity set, not a confidence interval, hard physical bound or
guarantee that future observations lie inside it. Treat transfer to unseen and
pre-VIIRS periods as a separate assumption to evaluate/disclose.

Do not add an arbitrary smoothness constraint just to make conclusions stable.
Do not enlarge discrepancy allowances merely to manufacture counterexamples.
Do not assign independent sensor factors to each month to create reversals.
If only independent interval bounds are justified, use interval arithmetic;
do not advertise an advanced optimizer as adding information.

Use a bounded linear formulation and scipy.optimize.linprog only if the actual
registered equations/constraints are linear. Otherwise simplify the model or
use verified analytic calculations. No neural solver or full physical inversion.
Record solver version, feasibility/optimality status and numerical tolerances.

7. OUTPUT LOGIC AND COUNTEREXAMPLE REQUIREMENTS

Stage A uses the outcomes stable_across_displayed_settings /
sensitive / unresolved, with UI text explicitly naming the registered calibration
variants. Missing required applicable evidence takes unresolved precedence;
retain any sensitivity already observed. inference_status stays not_established.
Do not rename finite-scenario agreement as continuous-model certification.

Mandatory independent output dimensions for every comparison:
- calibration_sensitivity: stable_across_displayed_settings / sensitive /
  unresolved, with registered variants and required-evidence reasons.
- applicability_status: evaluated_for_conditions / outside_evaluated_conditions /
  insufficient_evidence; include region, time, sensor, policy, support, tests,
  version IDs and pass/fail/limited findings. Evaluated does NOT mean passed.
- inference_status: not_established unless a separately justified statistical
  inference procedure is explicitly authorized. Do not collapse these dimensions
  into a reassuring single badge or report a directional conclusion when its
  mandatory applicability evidence fails or is absent.

Decision relevance - applies to Stage A and Stage B:
Register any scientifically justified nonnegative practical margin tau and its
absolute effect units BEFORE evaluating the outcomes. For each scenario use
Delta > tau for meaningful increase, Delta < -tau for meaningful decrease and
-tau <= Delta <= tau for within the practical margin. Report the category as
consistent only if all required scenarios and evidence are available and agree.
Track directional sign changes separately from practical-category changes: small
opposite signs can fall within the same practical margin. 'Within the margin'
means neither statistical equivalence nor zero real-world effect. Do not invent
an arbitrary default tau; if none is defensible, show magnitude and sign, with
practical importance unspecified. Missing data, zero baselines, ties, rounding
and percentage versus absolute effects require explicit handling. No category
alone creates a statistical-support claim.

The following extrema and additional statuses apply only to Stage B. Keep its
method ID and outputs distinct; no silent migration of the original audit schema.

For each compatible constraint set Theta, compute extrema of the registered
effect Delta(theta). A practical-effect threshold, if needed by the user task,
is registered before outcomes; numerical solver tolerance is a separate concept.

Possible statuses:
- conditional_invariant: the full verified feasible set satisfies the precise
  directional claim, with the declared margin/tolerance.
- ambiguous: feasible interpretations disagree on the registered conclusion.
- unavailable: inputs, applicable constraints or a reliable computation are
  missing; lack of computation is not demonstrated ambiguity.
- inconsistent: no interpretation satisfies the constraints. This is model/data
  inconsistency, not vacuous proof that a claim holds.

Keep inference_status=not_established by default. Mathematical invariance within
a chosen set is distinct from statistically calibrated inference about nature.
This proposal does not enable the old inferential supported label.

For ambiguity, return two complete parameter assignments and resulting monthly
series. They must satisfy all constraints simultaneously; do not assemble a
calendar from incompatible marginal extremes. Recompute witness feasibility
and claim outcomes in an independent checker before export.

Prefer two witnesses under the same policy to isolate model ambiguity. When
the disagreement is between support policies, label it policy sensitivity and
show both footprints/denominators. Do not imply that they estimate identical
sample populations or hide missing required variants.

Peak-month analysis:
- Month m is a possible peak only if a single feasible assignment satisfies
  Z_m >= Z_j for every other eligible month j simultaneously.
- A unique invariant peak requires strictly positive lower differences against
  every competitor beyond the registered numerical tolerance.
- Handle ties explicitly. Show the set of possible peak months when no unique
  peak is established; this is a useful partial answer, not an exact-week claim.
- If a required month is unavailable, do not claim a full-season peak by silently
  dropping it. A peak among observed eligible months needs that narrower label.

If every VIIRS month shares the same positive scalar and no other changing
terms, rankings must be preserved. The system must recognize this algebraic
fact rather than promote it as a new empirical discovery.

8. THE ENHANCEMENT: EXPLAIN WHICH ASSUMPTION MATTERS

Every result carries an assumption ledger:
observed / empirically evaluated / externally supported / assumed.

Stage A lists the tested factors, exclusions and affected comparisons. Stage B
may display registered nested relaxations where scientifically coherent. Show the
first relaxation at which the conclusion ceases to be invariant and include
its counterexample. This attributes fragility to a specific modeling assumption,
not a generic reliability score.

For the simple fixed cross-sensor comparison, an analytic equality threshold is
useful. For multiple parameters, a 'minimum bias' requires a registered metric
and scaling; there is no unique smallest perturbation without that definition.
Prefer named relaxation levels over an unjustified universal robustness number.

Stage A equality boundary - required where applicable:
For a fixed Aqua-reference value B and raw VIIRS value V, define the displayed
effect as Delta(a)=a*V-B. For V>0, equality occurs at a_break=B/V; increasing a
increases this effect. Do not state that exceeding the threshold necessarily
turns an increase into a decrease. Reversing the subtraction reverses the sign
convention; using an inverse efficiency parameter changes the interpretation.
For a registered material boundary d, solve Delta(a)=d, not Delta(a)=0.

Show the point estimate and each actual registered factor, their corresponding
effects and whether they straddle equality. A threshold within their min-max
envelope is not a tested probability, physical plausibility bound or confidence
statement. Interpolated slider values remain educational counterfactuals.
For V=0 there is no division-based threshold: Delta=-B for every valid a; if
B=0 too, every valid factor yields equality. Handle missing inputs, nonpositive
factors and out-of-domain comparisons explicitly. Both sides scaled by the same
positive factor have no factor-induced directional reversal. Recompute B/V for
each applicable policy and do not reuse it when baseline/support changes.
Test sign orientation, ties, zero/missing values and full-precision arithmetic.

9. EVALUATION THAT CAN REJECT THE IDEA

Baselines use identical data, eligibility, target and train/test separation:
A. Raw sensor comparison, clearly limited.
B. Point harmonization: apply the full-training a_H factor above to VIIRS,
   then compute the same period effect as A; no variant-based warning.
C. Block-bootstrap interval conditional on the fitted factor: resample registered
   geographic blocks with replacement jointly across both periods and sensors,
   recompute pooled D/O and the effect with the frozen training factor, and take
   registered percentile endpoints. Freeze replicate count, confidence level,
   seed, minimum independent blocks and practical margin tau at kickoff. Abstain
   unless the interval lies wholly above +tau or wholly below -tau; missing
   support or too few blocks is unavailable. Report within-margin separately;
   do not infer independence from correlated blocks or tune on holdouts. This
   interval does not include uncertainty in the fitted calibration factor.
D. Stage A finite registered-factor audit defined in Section 6.
E. THE THIRD EYE joint constraints and counterexamples.

Before claiming superiority over uncertainty-aware harmonization generally,
include a separately registered baseline that propagates training-factor
uncertainty and preserves shared dependencies. If independent training support
is inadequate, report that baseline unavailable and narrow the comparative claim;
do not manufacture precision or tune it using holdouts.

Evaluate Stage A against A-C. Evaluate E against A-D only if Stage B passes its
development gate. Clearly distinguish a method not implemented from a method
tested and found ineffective. Do not claim improvement merely from adding badges.

Compare uncertainty on the effect itself with shared dependencies respected;
overlap of two marginal intervals is not the only or preferred baseline test.
Do not handicap baselines by forcing them to answer every comparison.

Report:
- Separate raw-to-point-calibrated changes from disagreement among calibrated
  variants. Report both directional and prespecified material-magnitude changes;
  consistent corrections can be useful even when all fitted variants agree.
- Outcome distribution for registered cross-era comparisons: stable, sensitive,
  unresolved required evidence, and outside evaluated applicability. Store
  applicability separately; count out-of-domain cases explicitly in the summary
  without double-counting them as in-domain unresolved cases. Give denominators.
- Report within-sensor comparisons separately. Fixed-positive-scalar ranking
  invariance applies only with unchanged policy, samples and weights; missing
  required evidence still blocks a complete result. Do not count algebraically
  guaranteed agreement as independent evidence of sensor-transfer quality.
- Reference-relative directional disagreement among decisive answers.
- Decisive-answer fraction over ALL registered eligible evaluation cases.
- Error/coverage trade-off at prespecified settings, including matched coverage
  where sample sizes permit. Do not choose the best test-set setting afterward.
- Reference peak inclusion and size of the possible-peak set.
- Constraint violations on held-out reference observations.
- Unavailable/inconsistent counts and reasons, stratified by season and year.
- Runtime and independently checked witness feasibility.
- A judge-readable top-line table: baseline and THE THIRD EYE share identical
  registered comparison cases and their denominators, actual disagreement rates,
  decisive coverage, abstentions, missing cases and data versions. No cherry-
  picked headline percentage and no claimed superiority if it is not measured.

Use 'reference-relative disagreement', not real-world false-fire-claim rate.
Same-sensor observations are imperfect and may share bias with the test series.
Compare temporal and spatial tests separately. Do not fabricate narrow error
bars from correlated cell-days. Small-sample results remain exploratory.

Sensitivity frequency is diagnostic, never a success quota. Zero reversals does
not imply failure: the corrected result may consistently differ from the raw
comparison or resolve a consequential question. Conversely, if raw, corrected
and variant outputs are practically identical and users gain no measurable
benefit, report weak differentiation. Do not widen allowances, change region or
select new claims to manufacture a visible reversal.

Synthetic tests with known constructed histories validate solver behavior,
invariance, ambiguity, empty sets, ties and missingness. Label them synthetic.
They do not validate historical observation assumptions.

9D. CONDITIONAL EVIDENCE REPAIR - ONE-BATCH ARCHIVE EXPERIMENT

Purpose: identify obtainable archived evidence that improves the defensibility
of a disputed cross-sensor conclusion. It may distinguish competing scenarios,
leave ambiguity intact, or challenge all represented scenarios. Model inadequacy
is a separate informative outcome, not a forced resolution of the historical
question. This is evidence selection, not reconstruction of unobserved fires
or satellite tasking.
Keep it inside the comparison/evidence record, not a fifth core deliverable.

Scope gate: the calendar, Stage A, mandatory handover experiment and reproducible
release must already work. There must be an ambiguous case selected under a
registered rule, a finite compatible evidence pool, and predictive interpretations
with development-checked discrepancy/noise allowances. No ambiguity means no
eligible demonstration, not permission to widen bounds or change the flagship.

P1/P2/P5 may use the six-person-hour optional diagnostic allocation in this plan,
instead of swath/persistence/composition research or another advanced extension.
P4 records named capacity and includes integration within that allocation. Start
by Sunday 13:00; by 18:00 retain only an independently reproduced experiment or
mark it experimental/omit it. P0 deadlines and geographical usability take
priority. Do not make Stage B plus Evidence Repair two mandatory research builds.

Minimum implementation:
- One candidate type: preregistered overlap-era block/period aggregates from
  event-acquired products, with compatible masks, support and quality metadata.
- One fixed-budget batch selection, one reveal, one frozen update rule. No
  adaptive multi-round policy, new dataset, online learner or archive crawler.
- Reuse event-built versioned artifacts and deterministic calculations; an API is optional. No LLM
  decides which measurements are scientifically admissible.

Important prerequisites:
Two numerical endpoints are not automatically competing predictive models.
Stage A leave-one-year-out factors are correlated stability variants, not an
exhaustive model population or equal-probability hypotheses. They may be studied
as finite scenarios only with explicit predictions and a registered update rule;
results then apply to that scenario set. Removing a variant does not establish
global identifiability. Do not promote data-policy alternatives with different
estimands into competing estimates of a single latent truth.

For each candidate, models must predict the SAME observable quantity under the
candidate's actual measurement/support definition. Predictive discrepancy and
measurement uncertainty must be learned/justified on development data; a single
noisy value cannot be treated as exact falsification. Evaluate the whole declared
set of opposite-conclusion scenarios, not just two convenient witnesses. If only
two are evaluated, explicitly report pairwise evidence selection, not resolution
of all ambiguity. If the model set is continuous, a grid search is approximate
unless an analytical or optimization guarantee is established.

Selection rule:
Register a simple deterministic score for separation between opposite-conclusion
predictive envelopes relative to discrepancy allowances and batch cost. Worst-case
separation across the represented opposing scenarios is one candidate rule;
freeze normalization, ties and abstention on development data. This is a heuristic
unless stronger properties are proved. Overlapping predictions may still contain
information; failure of this conservative score is not proof of uselessness.
No 'expected improvement' probability without a justified outcome distribution.

Algebraic novelty/futility gate BEFORE an Evidence Repair build:
For the Stage A scalar family Z=a*V, two scenario factors a1 and a2 predict
separation |a1*V-a2*V| = |a1-a2|*V for nonnegative V. If all candidates have the
same factor pair, noise/discrepancy allowance and cost, maximizing raw predictive
separation is mathematically identical to choosing the largest permitted V.
In that case a more elaborate score does not create a distinct selection method.
Under heterogeneous costs/uncertainties, derive the actual ranking formula and
test whether it is still order-equivalent to highest pre-reveal activity or a
simple normalized-activity heuristic. Do not infer nonlinearity from labels.

P1 records this symbolic reduction, score assumptions and a small hand-computed
counterexample/fixture before implementing an optimizer. On development data,
measure top-candidate agreement and ranking overlap with highest permitted
activity, least support and uncertainty-aware selection. If all candidate rankings
are equivalent under registered conditions, stop the distinctive Evidence Repair
claim; a useful simple retrieval tool may remain, explicitly identified as such.

An extension is justified only by externally or development-supported differences
in measurement quality, predictive discrepancy, feasible acquisition cost or
non-equivalent models, with matched observable targets. Do not add artificial
heterogeneity merely to manufacture a better ranking. Even a distinct ranking
must show higher independent reference-relative accuracy or useful coverage at
matched evidence cost in the blind test. Novelty is not established by different
buttons, a sophisticated solver, or shrinking an uncertainty set alone.

Costs are declared units such as granules, bytes or analyst minutes. Count real
processing cost if features must be extracted to rank a candidate. 'Minimum
evidence' is permitted only if exhaustive search/valid optimization certifies a
minimum over the stated finite pool and cost definition, including the outcome
criterion. Default wording: 'highest-ranked candidate within the tested pool'.
Before observing a batch, its utility is conditional; no guarantee of resolution.

Blind Evidence Recovery Challenge:
1. Freeze three disjoint roles: development/model fitting, hidden candidate
   acquisition pool, and independent final reference cases. Use blocked time/
   geography units to limit dependence. Reserve these before inspection; earlier
   disclosed test results cannot become a new blind pool by relabeling them.
2. P5 withholds candidate outcome values from the selector. Publish an allowlist
   of pre-reveal metadata/predictors and their cost. For example, a known VIIRS
   predictor and withheld Aqua response is allowed only if explicitly designed
   that way; do not claim both measurements were hidden. Do not rank on hidden
   fire counts, QA derived from hidden labels or leakage through cached summaries.
3. Each selection method receives identical visible inputs, feasible pool and
   budget. Compare random selection over fixed repeated seeds, least-supported
   selection, highest permitted pre-reveal observed/predicted activity, a simple
   cost/noise-normalized activity ranking where supported, and a strong
   uncertainty-based selector. Never use the hidden response to define 'highest
   fire'. Include a no-acquisition baseline, and disclose when Evidence Repair is
   algebraically identical to any comparator.
4. Reveal only the selected batch to each method and apply the SAME frozen
   fitting/compatibility update rule. Do not add a different model to rescue the
   preferred selector. Test whether data contradict ALL scenarios; return model
   inconsistency rather than forcing a surviving answer. Unmodeled shared bias
   can survive every comparison and remains a limitation.
5. Evaluate before/after decisions on the disjoint final reference cases. Selected
   outcomes become adaptation data, not independent validation. Keep original
   benchmark artifacts immutable; updated models/results get new versions and
   explicitly limited applicability. Relevance to earlier history remains assumed
   unless independently justified; new observations do not recover missed 2010 fires.

Evidence Repair must also test whether the revealed batch contradicts ALL
represented scenarios under the frozen measurement/noise compatibility rule.
This is a model-inadequacy warning, not a selected winning interpretation or
proof of a historical direction. Report these flags separately from successful
ambiguity resolution; account for potential false flags and newly wrong decisive
answers. A single surprising measurement is not automatic model falsification.

Measure reference-relative error at matched evidence cost and useful answer
coverage; count newly wrong decisive answers, correct resolutions, abstentions,
model inconsistency and no-benefit selections. Narrower sets alone are not success.
Report all registered cases, null outcomes, cost accounting and dependence/small
sample limits. One anecdote demonstrates mechanics, not comparative superiority.
Acquiring more archived data helps establish retrieval efficiency, not improved
satellite observation coverage or real-world detection recall.

Evidence Repair Record, attached to the event-built comparison:
Disputed claim/estimand and version; source of ambiguity; represented scenarios;
candidate IDs and permitted pre-reveal fields; predictive differences/discrepancy
allowances; score/cost; availability and quality status; update rule; selected
batch and revealed provenance; before/after results; independent evaluation;
historical-transfer limitations and reproduction command.

Use precise evidence-status labels:
- candidate_available: archived metadata/access verified, informative potential
  under the declared models; actual usable retrieval/reveal may still fail.
- further_validation_may_help: testing transfer could help but no verified batch
  is presently available; future observations are not historical replacements.
- no_discriminating_candidate_found: the registered search/scoring rule found
  none in the feasible pool. State search coverage and whether heuristic/exact;
  this is not impossibility for all evidence, all combinations or future methods.
- after reveal: resolved_within_tested_set / still_ambiguous / inconsistent /
  unavailable, separately from reference-relative accuracy and inference status.

Do not use 'repaired evidence' to imply NASA records were changed. Evidence Repair
means revising a qualified interpretation after additional analysis. Keep the
original data, audit and report accessible. Release it as a demonstrated benefit
only if the blind comparison improves independent reference-relative results at
matched cost and useful answer coverage over both highest-activity and strong
uncertainty-aware selection under frozen criteria. Report ranking equivalence,
negative results, all costs and limited sample sizes. Otherwise retain the core,
mark the feature experimental, and make no claim of selection originality.
